# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project welcoming and free
from harassment for everyone. We will treat contributors and community
members with respect, regardless of their background, identity, experience,
or personal characteristics.

## Our Standards

We encourage participants to:

- Communicate with courtesy and empathy.
- Respect different perspectives and experiences.
- Offer constructive feedback and receive it openly.
- Acknowledge mistakes, make amends, and learn from them.
- Act in the best interests of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, personal attacks,
discriminatory or sexualized remarks, unwanted attention, and sharing
someone else's private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for applying this Code of Conduct fairly.
They may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation
when necessary. Where appropriate, they will explain moderation decisions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, and other community channels. It also applies when
someone officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [INSERT CONTACT EMAIL].
Maintainers will review reports promptly, respond fairly, and protect the
reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider the behavior, its impact, and any previous incidents
when deciding on a response. Possible actions include:

1. **Correction:** A private explanation of the problem and a request to
   change the behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning that specifies the behavior that must stop
   and the consequences of further violations.
3. **Temporary ban:** A time-limited restriction on participation in project
   spaces.
4. **Permanent ban:** Removal from the community for severe or repeated
   violations.
