# Simple Interest Calculator

This calculator computes simple interest and the total amount payable.

## Inputs

- Principal: the initial amount of money
- Annual interest rate: the rate as a percentage
- Time: the number of years

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

## Example

For a principal of 1,000, an annual rate of 5%, and a time of 2 years:

- Simple interest: 100
- Total amount: 1,100
